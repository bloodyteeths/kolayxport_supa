## The "my shop has everything" problem

A common trap for Turkish Etsy sellers is widening the range by guesswork: some rugs, some cushions, some jewellery. The result is a scattered shop with listings that aren't strong enough in any product group. If a shop that opened after yours in the same category makes hundreds of sales while your 150 products stand still, the difference is often in product choice.

## What's in KolayXport Research

The **product finder** shows what sells within a product group on Etsy, eBay and Trendyol, and at what prices.

The **keyword explorer** helps you find the phrases buyers actually type on Etsy and eBay. You then carry those words into your titles and tags.

With the **competitor shop tracker** you add Etsy and eBay competitors to a watch list; each shop's progress is recorded daily.

The **listing doctor** compares your own listing with similar listings from the same search and shows what's missing.

The **arbitrage table** matches Trendyol products with similar eBay listings and shows the price gap. Start here if you're thinking of sourcing on Trendyol to sell abroad, or you want to know how your own Trendyol products are priced internationally. This tool is part of Pro and open during the trial.

If a result is hard to read, ask AI to explain it: why is this product selling, and how is your listing different?

## From research to listing

Research on its own doesn't do much; you need to turn what you learn into a listing. Open the Etsy or eBay listing studio for the product you've chosen, and AI drafts the title, description and tags around the keywords you found. A while later, check the product in the listing doctor again to see how far you've closed the gap with competitors.

## What we don't do

- No Amazon research in the app; the numbers the extension shows on Amazon pages are estimates.
- The Chrome extension isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet.
- The arbitrage table doesn't calculate profit; you add shipping, fees and duties.
- Some shop tracker alerts aren't on yet, and the number of watched shops is limited.
- Etsy research shares Etsy's daily API quota.
