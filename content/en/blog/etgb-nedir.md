If you ship Etsy, eBay or Amazon orders abroad from Turkey, you will run into the same question sooner or later: "Do I get the ETGB, does the carrier issue it, and what does my accountant need?" This guide explains what the ETGB is, who files it, how to look up its number and where VAT refunds most often get stuck, with official sources.

> This article is general information, not legal or tax advice. Check your own situation with your accountant (mali müşavir) and your carrier.

## What is ETGB?

ETGB stands for Elektronik Ticaret Gümrük Beyannamesi, the e-commerce customs declaration. It is a simplified electronic declaration used instead of a full export declaration for small export shipments sent by post or express courier. Since 2022 the legal name is **"basitleştirilmiş gümrük beyannamesi"** (simplified customs declaration), but VAT refund screens and everyday usage still say ETGB ([Communiqué No. 1, Official Gazette 22.04.2022](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2022/04/20220422-5.htm)).

You don't file it yourself. The express carrier (the "operator") or PTT files it on your behalf as an **indirect representative**. Your job is to give the carrier correct information.

## The 2026 ETGB limit: 600 kg and €30,000

Communiqué No. 5, published in the **Official Gazette of 3 January 2026**, raised the export limit for ETGB to **600 kg** gross and **€30,000**, up from 300 kg and €15,000. It took effect on the day of publication ([Communiqué No. 5](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2026/01/20260103-4.htm), [KPMG](https://kpmgvergi.com/yayinlar/mali-bultenler/gumruk/posta-ve-hizli-kargo-yoluyla-tasinan-esyanin-gumruk-islemlerine-iliskin-tebligde-degisiklik-yapildi/3455)). Some sites give 4 December 2025 as the start date. We go by the Official Gazette date.

Shipments above the limit need a regular export declaration, usually filed through a customs broker. See also our [2026 micro-export guide](/en/blog/mikro-ihracat-rehberi-2026).

## What to give your carrier

The declaration is based on the information and documents the carrier holds, so missing or wrong data goes straight into the ETGB.

- **Invoice.** An e-Arşiv or e-Fatura invoice addressed to the foreign buyer. Amount and currency must match the declaration.
- **HS code (GTİP).** Since 1 July 2025 export ETGBs need **at least 6 digits** ([Communiqué No. 3](https://orgtr.org/posta-ve-hizli-kargo-yoluyla-tasinan-esyanin-gumruk-islemlerine-iliskin-teblig-seri-no-1de-degisiklik-yapilmasina-dair-teblig-seri-no-3/)). VAT refund lists ask for **12 digits**, so keep the full code in your records.
- **Description and value**, written the same way as on the invoice.
- **Marketplace.** If the sale came through Etsy, eBay or Amazon, the carrier enters the marketplace name and tax number in the declaration's "Pazaryeri" field ([Customs guide, 2025](https://ggm.ticaret.gov.tr/data/682c924713b8762dd8da8763/Basitle%C5%9Ftirilmi%C5%9F%20G%C3%BCmr%C3%BCk%20Beyannamesi%20Y%C3%BCk%C3%BCml%C3%BC%20Beyan%20K%C4%B1lavuzu.pdf)). Tell your carrier where each order came from.

Carriers may also ask for an authorisation letter, an undertaking or their own shipment forms. These differ by carrier, so get the current list from yours.

## How to get and look up the ETGB number

The carrier registers the declaration before the shipment leaves. After registration it gets a number containing **"EX"**, for example `26341300EX123456`. Once the flight departs and the exit is confirmed, the declaration becomes **"closed"** ([customs guide](https://ggm.ticaret.gov.tr/data/682c924713b8762dd8da8763/Basitle%C5%9Ftirilmi%C5%9F%20G%C3%BCmr%C3%BCk%20Beyannamesi%20Y%C3%BCk%C3%BCml%C3%BC%20Beyan%20K%C4%B1lavuzu.pdf), [GİB guide](https://intvrg.gib.gov.tr/kdv_iadesi_duyuru_yeni.pdf)).

- Your carrier sends you the ETGB number and document. How and how fast differs by carrier, so ask before your first shipment.
- You can check the closing date on the Ministry of Trade's [declaration lookup](https://uygulama.gtb.gov.tr/BeyannameSorgulama/) by choosing "ETGB" and entering the number.
- Corrections are only possible within **24 hours** after departure, and no new waybill can be added. Tell your carrier at once if you spot an error.

## ETGB and VAT refunds

Export deliveries are exempt from VAT, and for courier exports the ETGB is the document that proves it ([VAT General Communiqué II/A-1.1.2](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/File/GeneratePdf?mevzuatNo=19631&mevzuatTur=Teblig&mevzuatTertip=5)). Three rules matter:

1. **Invoice and ETGB must match.** If the amount, currency or item differs, the tax office asks customs for information and the refund is delayed.
2. **The period follows the ETGB's closing date, not the invoice date.** The export goes into the VAT return for the period that contains the closing date, converted at that day's central bank buying rate.
3. **List details matter.** GİB's lists take the ETGB's own currency and amount, a 12-digit HS code without dots, and for e-Arşiv invoices an empty series field with the full 16-character document number ([GİB guide](https://intvrg.gib.gov.tr/kdv_iadesi_duyuru_yeni.pdf)).

Offset refunds need no report or guarantee. Cash refunds up to 50,000 TL need none either.

## Who gets the 50% income deduction?

Under Income Tax Law article 89/1-16, **resident individuals** exporting with ETGB may deduct **50%** of the profit from those sales ([Income Tax Law, July 2026 consolidated text](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.4.193.pdf)). Conditions:

- You must be insured yourself in that year.
- Staff requirements by yearly ETGB export revenue: none up to 400,000 TL, at least 1 employee up to 800,000 TL, 2 up to 1,600,000 TL, 3 up to 2,400,000 TL.
- Above 2,400,000 TL the deduction is lost entirely.
- Companies (corporate taxpayers) can't use it, and exports under a regular customs declaration don't qualify.

These amounts can be changed by presidential decree, so check the current figures with your accountant.

## Quick checklist

- Invoice the foreign buyer, VAT-free, with the same amount and currency as the shipment.
- Keep each product's 12-digit HS code and give the carrier at least 6 digits.
- Tell the carrier which marketplace each order came from.
- Send your accountant the ETGB numbers and closing dates every month with your invoices.
- Report any error to the carrier within 24 hours.
