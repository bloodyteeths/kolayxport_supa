## In short

Sentos is an integrator with a broad list of Turkish marketplace, carrier and accounting integrations. On its [pricing page](https://www.sentos.com.tr/fiyatlar/), packages are priced annually, and the e-export integrations that include Etsy sit in the Platinum package (₺89,000/year). eBay is not in the marketplace list on its [Etsy integration page](https://www.sentos.com.tr/etsy-entegrasyonu/).

KolayXport is built for sellers who sell abroad: single and bulk editing of Etsy and eBay listings (eBay listings created in Seller Hub excepted), AI for English titles and tags, keyword and product research, and international FedEx/UPS labels. Its domestic coverage is narrow: no Hepsiburada, N11 or Çiçeksepeti, and domestic labels only through DHL eCommerce (MNG).

## Who should pick which?

**Sentos may suit you better if:**
- most of your sales are on Hepsiburada, N11 or Çiçeksepeti,
- you need labels from carriers such as Yurtiçi or Aras,
- you want a deep connection to your accounting or ERP software.

**KolayXport may suit you better if:**
- your main channels are Etsy and/or eBay,
- your listings aren't getting seen and you need English titles, tags and research tools,
- you prefer a monthly subscription to paying a year up front.

**Using both:** you can keep domestic marketplaces in your current integrator and run Etsy and eBay in KolayXport. If you do, run stock sync in only one tool; two tools writing the same product's stock causes confusion.

Prices and package contents change. The Sentos details on this page were checked on Sentos's own pages on 17 October 2026. For KolayXport's current plans, see the [pricing page](/en/fiyatlandirma).
