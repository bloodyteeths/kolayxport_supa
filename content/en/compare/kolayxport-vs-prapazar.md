## In short

PraPazar and KolayXport overlap a lot: both pull Etsy and eBay orders and both offer international shipping labels. The differences are in coverage and listing tools.

PraPazar's [package page](https://prapazar.com/tr/paketler) lists 24 Turkish marketplaces plus international ones such as AliExpress, Ozon, Allegro and Temu. Cargo integration is a +₺13,000 add-on and e-invoice integrations are +₺1,850–6,500. The monthly option is the Eko plan (₺2,550/month); other plans are annual. Its [Etsy page](https://prapazar.com/tr/entegrasyon/etsy-entegrasyonu) describes orders, invoices and labels, price/stock updates and multilingual listings with automatic descriptions.

In KolayXport you can edit Etsy listings in full (photos, video, variations, tags) and eBay listings (except those created in Seller Hub), one at a time or in bulk, keep the changes as drafts and send them when you're ready. There's also AI for English titles and tags, a listing doctor, and keyword and product research. Marketplace coverage is narrower: Trendyol is the only Turkish marketplace; abroad it's Etsy, eBay, Amazon (orders), Shopify and Wix.

## Who should pick which?

**PraPazar may suit you better if:**
- you sell on many Turkish marketplaces, or on channels such as Allegro, Ozon or Temu,
- you use accounting software other than Paraşüt,
- what you mainly need is the order, invoice and stock flow.

**KolayXport may suit you better if:**
- listing quality and visibility on Etsy and eBay are your priority,
- you want bulk editing, AI and research in one place,
- you don't want to pay an add-on for international labels (you connect your own FedEx/UPS account).

The PraPazar details were checked on PraPazar's own pages on 17 October 2026; prices change. For KolayXport's current plans, see the [pricing page](/en/fiyatlandirma).
