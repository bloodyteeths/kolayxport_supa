## If your Trendyol margin keeps shrinking, widen your buyer base

Between commission, the shipping barem and ads, what's left on Trendyol keeps getting thinner. Selling the same products abroad in dollars is a sensible next step for many makers. But it means solving two things at once: an English listing that people actually find, and stock that doesn't get mixed up between channels.

## Trendyol and international, side by side

**Your products in one place.** Create, edit and update price and stock for your Trendyol products in KolayXport. Changes stay as drafts and go to Trendyol when you press Send. Turkish AI writes titles and descriptions for Trendyol.

**Check what sells first.** The arbitrage table matches Trendyol products with similar eBay listings and shows the price gap. The product finder shows what sells on Etsy, eBay and Trendyol. You decide which product to take abroad based on data, not guesswork.

**Write the listing in English.** Open an Etsy or eBay listing in the listing studio and AI fills in the English title, description and tags. A listing you create on Etsy can be copied to eBay in one click.

**Stock from one pool.** In the stock sync wizard you match your Trendyol, Etsy, eBay and Shopify products by SKU. A dry run shows what would change before you go live. After that, a sale on one channel lowers stock on the others.

## Think about price and profit separately

Converting your Trendyol price to dollars isn't enough. Etsy and eBay fees, international shipping and returns all belong in the calculation. The profit report shows Etsy, eBay, Amazon and Trendyol sales with commission, shipping, ads and the product costs you enter, so you can see side by side which channel actually earns. The profit report is a Pro feature and is open during the trial.

## What we don't do

- We don't integrate with Hepsiburada, N11 or Çiçeksepeti. Think of KolayXport as an addition for your international channels, not a replacement for your current integrator.
- No direct listing copy from Trendyol to Etsy/eBay; the reverse direction exists.
- We don't send tracking numbers to Trendyol orders.
- With Trendyol and eBay, two sales arriving at the same moment can occasionally collide during a stock update, which is one reason stock sync is offered as beta.
- International FedEx or UPS shipping needs your own carrier account.
