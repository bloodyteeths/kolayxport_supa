## The hard part on eBay isn't listing, it's listing the right thing well

eBay buyers search in English and filter by category-specific item specifics. A listing with a short title and missing specifics falls behind in search. For a seller in Turkey, the second challenge is knowing what to sell: what do people search for on eBay, and at what price does it sell?

## How eBay works in KolayXport

**Research.** The product finder and keyword explorer show what's searched for and sold on eBay. If you sell on Trendyol, the arbitrage table shows the price gap for similar products on eBay.

**Listing.** Enter your product details in the listing studio and AI fills in the English title, description, item specifics and a price suggestion. Publish right away, or leave it on eBay unpublished. Already selling on Etsy? Copy a listing to an eBay draft in one click; photos and variations come along and you set the price.

**Updates.** Change prices and quantities in one grid in the bulk editor. Every change stays a draft until you press Send.

**Orders and shipping.** eBay orders appear alongside your other channels. Print international labels with your own FedEx or UPS account, and the tracking number is sent to the eBay order.

## If you already sell on Etsy

The quickest start is to copy the Etsy listings that already sell well to eBay. A copied listing doesn't go live on its own; it waits as a draft. Check the eBay category and item specifics, set a price based on similar eBay listings, then send it. If the same product sells on both channels, turn on stock sync so a sale on one lowers stock on the other.

## Limits to know about

- Older listings created in eBay Seller Hub can't be edited in KolayXport, only copied into a new listing. Stock sync skips them too.
- eBay messages aren't shown in the app.
- FedEx and UPS labels are for international shipments only and need your own carrier account. We don't track FedEx/UPS shipment status in the app; the number is stored and sent to eBay.
- Software can't help with eBay's own decisions, such as account suspensions.
