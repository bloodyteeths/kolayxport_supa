On Etsy Community's Turkish sellers forum, one seller asks: ["4 aylık Etsy mağazam, 134 ürün, 4 satış — bu normal mi?"](https://community.etsy.com/forum/etsy-t%C3%BCrk-sat%C4%B1c%C4%B1lar%C4%B1-95/topic/4-ayl%C4%B1k-etsy-ma%C4%9Fazam-134-%C3%BCr%C3%BCn-4-sat%C4%B1%C5%9F-bu-normal-mi-deneyimli-sat%C4%B1c%C4%B1lar%C4%B1n-g%C3%B6r%C3%BC%C5%9F%C3%BCn%C3%BC-rica-ediyorum-ilk-%C3%BCr%C3%BCn-2-ay-%C3%B6nce-eklendi-187161/) ("My Etsy shop is 4 months old: 134 listings, 4 sales. Is this normal?") Similar threads are common there: "no sales for 19 days", "I run ads but clicks don't turn into sales".

There is no single reason a shop doesn't sell. Product, price, season and competition all matter. What you control directly is your listings. The 10 checks below are meant to be done before you put more money into ads. None of them guarantees sales; the goal is to remove the gaps that stop buyers from finding you, or from understanding what you sell once they do.

## 1. Title: the item first, key traits right after

In its Seller Handbook, Etsy recommends clearly stating the item, putting the most important traits (colour, material, size) up front and keeping titles under 15 words ([Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697); summary at [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-guidance-listing-titles-ai/)). The same guidance says to move subjective words like "perfect" or "beautiful" to the description, avoid repeating words, and leave price, shipping and sale details out of the title.

Check: can someone who reads only the first four or five words tell what you sell?

## 2. Tags: are all 13 used, and are they different?

Each listing gets 13 tags of up to 20 characters each ([Outfy, Etsy character limits](https://www.outfy.com/blog/etsy-character-limit/)). Every empty tag is a search you could have matched and didn't. Use the two- or three-word phrases buyers actually type rather than single words, and cover different intents (use, material, style, recipient) instead of repeating one word across tags.

## 3. Category and attributes: complete?

The right category and its attributes (colour, material, room, style, size) help your listing appear in filtered searches. When a buyer filters by "colour: blue", a listing without a colour may not show. Bulk-uploaded or copied listings often have these fields empty.

## 4. Photo count and video

Etsy lets you add up to 20 photos and one 5–15 second video per listing ([Etsy Help: Listing a Physical Item](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628707-Listing-a-Physical-Item)). Buyers can't touch the item, so photos are their only information: scale (in hand, in a room, on a model), details (stitching, texture, clasp), colour variants and packaging all answer questions before they are asked.

## 5. First photo: does it work as a thumbnail?

In search, buyers see only a small crop of your first photo. Open it on a phone at thumbnail size. Is the item clear, does the background distract, does it fit the square? Next to competitors' thumbnails for the same search, does yours stand out?

## 6. Price and shipping profile: realistic?

For sellers shipping from Turkey, shipping is a large share of the price. Make sure the processing and delivery times in your shipping profile are realistic, because late orders affect reviews and shop performance. Before setting prices, work out what is left after fees, shipping, ads and currency; see our [Etsy profit calculation post](/en/blog/etsy-kar-hesaplama).

## 7. The first lines of the description

Few buyers read the whole description. The first paragraph should say what the item is, its size and material, who it suits and how personalisation works. Story, care instructions and policies can sit further down.

## 8. Shop focus: is it clear what you sell?

One reply to a struggling seller on the same forum read: ["Etsy mağazayı spam algılıyor olabilir. Mağazada her şey var. Halı, yastık, runner."](https://community.etsy.com/forum/etsy-t%C3%BCrk-sat%C4%B1c%C4%B1lar%C4%B1-95/topic/b%C3%BCy%C3%BCk-bir-eme%C4%9Fin-kar%C5%9F%C4%B1l%C4%B1ks%C4%B1z-kalmas%C4%B1-165946/) ("Etsy may read the shop as spam. There's everything in it: rugs, cushions, runners.") That is a community opinion, not an Etsy statement, but the point stands: a shop of unrelated product groups can struggle to earn trust. Organise sections and make your main product group clear in the shop title and About section.

## 9. Turn on ads after fixing listings

The first seller also wrote: ["Reklamı ROAS 1,2 ile döndürüyorum, yani zarar."](https://community.etsy.com/forum/etsy-t%C3%BCrk-sat%C4%B1c%C4%B1lar%C4%B1-95/topic/4-ayl%C4%B1k-etsy-ma%C4%9Fazam-134-%C3%BCr%C3%BCn-4-sat%C4%B1%C5%9F-bu-normal-mi-deneyimli-sat%C4%B1c%C4%B1lar%C4%B1n-g%C3%B6r%C3%BC%C5%9F%C3%BCn%C3%BC-rica-ediyorum-ilk-%C3%BCr%C3%BCn-2-ay-%C3%B6nce-eklendi-187161/) ("I'm running ads at a ROAS of 1.2, so at a loss.") Ads show your listing to more people; if the listing doesn't convince those who click, the budget doesn't become sales. Fix the points above first, then test ads on a few of your strongest listings, and include ad spend in your profit math.

## 10. Measure and compare

Etsy's shop stats show which listings get views, which searches bring traffic and which listings get clicks without sales. Low views usually point to titles, tags and attributes; views without sales point to photos, price and description. Change things in small batches so you can see what worked, and put your listing next to the top results for the same search.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long until the first Etsy sale?

There is no standard timeframe; category, price, competition and season make a big difference. Views and clicks are more useful signals: no views means work on findability, views without sales means work on the listing itself.

### Should my tags be in Turkish or English?

In the language your buyers search in. If you target buyers abroad on Etsy, titles and tags should be in English.

### Should I turn ads off?

Ads aren't good or bad on their own. If listings aren't ready, the budget buys clicks that may not convert. Fixing listings first and then testing with a limited budget makes the result easier to read.

### Can I use AI-written titles as they are?

Treat them as a starting point. Check that size, material and personalisation details are correct before sending. In KolayXport suggestions land as drafts, so you can edit them first.
