## Style (Atilla, 2026-10-21)

All drafts and anything you write yourself in a DM: **2 to 4 sentences**, natural language, no dashes as punctuation (—, – or " - "), no dash bullets, no bold, no stock openers. Open with the concrete thing you read or watched.

## Before you send (every message)

1. **Give value first:** in a group, write ≥3 helpful answers (no links) over a few days; for a channel, watch the referenced video/post. Gameplan §4.5 rule.
2. **Check the hook is still true:** open the linked post or video. If it's gone or older than you think, change the line. Facebook groups were login-walled during research, so their first lines are generic until you've joined and read the group: replace `[son konu]` with a real recent topic.
3. **Check the tracking first:** don't send a ref link until it works. Test `kolayxport.com/?ref=CODE` in a private window (README checklist). These first messages contain **no link** on purpose.
4. **No prices, no income promises, no Hepsiburada/N11 integration claims.** Answers to questions are in `ortak-sss.md`.
5. **Log every message:** set Status in the table and fill the same row in the ERP "Partner pipeline" tab. Follow-ups after +4/+9/+16 days come from `../social/outreach-messages.md`.

Research for the hooks: public pages only, checked 2026-10-20. Sources are listed per row. Titles spot-checked by curl: E3, M6, M9, M10.
