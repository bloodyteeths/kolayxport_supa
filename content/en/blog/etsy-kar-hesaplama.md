Etsy charged the buyer about 200 lira… I was left with 99 lira.") The gap surprises many sellers because Etsy's deductions aren't one commission rate: transaction, payment processing, regulatory, currency conversion, ads and shipping all come off separately.

This post lists the deductions for an Etsy seller based in Turkey, then works through a hypothetical order step by step. Rates change, so for your own numbers rely on the breakdown in your Etsy Payments account.

## Etsy fees for sellers in Turkey (October 2026)

These rates are compiled from secondary sources based on Etsy's [Fees & Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees). We couldn't access Etsy's page directly, so we cross-checked [Global Fee Calculator's Turkey page](https://globalfeecalculator.com/etsy-fee-turkey/) (May 2026) and [Listybox's 2026 guide](https://listybox.com/tr/blog/what-percentage-does-etsy-take-2026/).

- **Listing fee:** USD 0.20 per listing, charged again when an item sells and the listing renews.
- **Transaction fee:** 6.5% of the order total, including shipping and gift wrap.
- **Payment processing (Turkey):** 6.5% + 14 TRY per order.
- **Regulatory operating fee:** sources differ between 2.24% and 2.27%; roughly 2.2–2.3%. Your Etsy Payments breakdown shows the exact rate.
- **Currency conversion:** 2.5% when your listing currency differs from your payment account currency (e.g. listings in USD, payouts in TRY).
- **Offsite Ads:** 15% on a sale attributed to Etsy's external ads. Shops under USD 10,000 in the last 12 months can opt out; above that, participation is mandatory at 12%.
- **Etsy Ads:** whatever budget you set; a separate cost.

On top of these come your own costs: product, packaging, actual shipping and any customs costs.

## A hypothetical order

The numbers below are **examples only**, not from a real shop. Swap in your own.

**Assumptions:**

- Item price USD 40, shipping charged to the buyer USD 10. Order total: **USD 50**.
- Listings in USD, payment account in TRY (currency conversion applies).
- Example rate: 1 USD ≈ 49.2 TRY ([Türkgün, 7 Oct 2026](https://www.turkgun.com/ekonomi/dolar-bugun-ne-kadar-7-ekim-2026-dolar-euro-kuru-kac-tl/405055)).
- Actual shipping cost USD 18, product and packaging USD 9, Etsy Ads spend for this sale USD 4.
- Regulatory fee at the upper value, 2.27%.

**1. Etsy deductions**

| Item | Calculation | Amount (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Listing fee | flat | 0.20 |
| Transaction fee | 50 × 6.5% | 3.25 |
| Payment processing | 50 × 6.5% + 14 TRY (≈ 0.28) | 3.53 |
| Regulatory fee | 50 × 2.27% | 1.14 |
| Currency conversion | 50 × 2.5% | 1.25 |
| **Total** | | **9.37** |

That is about 18.7% of the order total.

**2. Your costs**

50 − 9.37 (Etsy) − 18 (shipping) − 9 (product) − 4 (Etsy Ads) = **USD 9.63**, about **474 TRY** at the example rate.

**3. If the sale came through Offsite Ads**

50 × 15% = USD 7.50 more. Left: 9.63 − 7.50 = **USD 2.13**, about **105 TRY**.

Same item, same price: depending on where the sale came from, what you keep can drop to under a quarter. Price with the worst case (Offsite Ads plus ads) in mind.

## Shipping and customs: new margin items in 2026

Shipping is often bigger than Etsy's fees, and 2026 added two customs changes:

- **US:** duty-free de minimis treatment for low-value shipments has been suspended since 29 August 2025, so low-value parcels also face duties and fees.
- **EU:** from 1 July 2026, parcels under €150 pay a flat €3 customs duty per tariff classification.

Whether you or the buyer pays depends on your shipping method and carrier. See our [2026 micro-export guide](/en/blog/mikro-ihracat-rehberi-2026) for details and sources, and don't set this line to zero.

## VAT, invoicing and FX conversion support

For micro-exports, e-Arşiv invoices are issued with 0% VAT under Article 11/1-a of the Turkish VAT Law, and input VAT may be refunded or offset ([Paraşüt](https://www.parasut.com/blog/ihracatta-e-fatura-earsiv-uygulamasi)). Agree with your accountant on the amount, exchange rate and exemption used on the invoice.

The central bank's FX conversion support rules also changed on 1 October 2026: the commitment not to buy foreign currency was dropped and replaced by criteria based on FX position and value added ([Forbes Türkiye](https://www.forbes.com.tr/ekonomi/tcmb-doviz-donusum-desteginde-yeni-donem-sartlar-degisiyor)). Check with your accountant whether it applies to you.

## A checklist for your own numbers

1. Download the last few months from your Etsy Payments account and split deductions by line.
2. Write down the real cost of each product (item, packaging, labels).
3. Compare actual shipping cost with what buyers pay for shipping.
4. Divide Etsy Ads spend by the number of ad-attributed sales.
5. Flag orders that came through Offsite Ads.
6. Account for currency movement between the sale date and payout date.
7. If you still make a profit in the worst case, your price is sustainable.

## Frequently asked questions

### What percentage does Etsy take in total?

There is no single rate. In the example above, transaction, payment, regulatory and currency conversion fees came to about 18.7% of the order total. A sale through Offsite Ads adds 12% or 15%. Your Etsy Payments breakdown shows your own figure.

### Is the transaction fee charged on shipping too?

Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee is calculated on the order total, including the shipping the buyer pays.

### Can I avoid the currency conversion fee?

It applies when your listing currency differs from your payment account currency. Weigh the effect on your prices and buyers before changing listing currency.

### Can I turn off Offsite Ads?

If your shop sold under USD 10,000 in the last 12 months, you can opt out. Above that, participation is mandatory at 12%.
