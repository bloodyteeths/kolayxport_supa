# Privacy Policy - Kolayxport Etsy Order Sync Extension

*Last Updated: January 22, 2025*

## Overview

The Kolayxport Etsy Order Sync Chrome Extension ("Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our Extension.

## Information Collection

### What We Access
The Extension accesses:
- Order information visible on your Etsy Shop Manager pages
- Buyer names and shipping addresses from your orders
- Order details including items, prices, and dates

### What We Don't Access
The Extension does NOT access:
- Your Etsy login credentials
- Your personal seller information
- Any buyer payment information
- Pages outside of Etsy Shop Manager Orders
- Any other browser tabs or websites

## Information Usage

### How We Use Information
- **Order Synchronization**: Transfer order data to your Kolayxport account
- **Duplicate Prevention**: Store order IDs locally to prevent re-syncing
- **Status Tracking**: Display sync statistics in the extension popup

### Data Transmission
- Order data is sent ONLY to your authenticated Kolayxport account
- All data transmission uses secure HTTPS encryption
- No data is sent to any third parties

## Data Storage

### Local Storage
The Extension stores:
- Previously synced order IDs (up to 5,000 most recent)
- Sync statistics (count and last sync time)
- Authentication status

This data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API.

### Server Storage
- Order data is stored in your Kolayxport account
- Subject to Kolayxport's main privacy policy
- No additional data is collected by the Extension itself

## Data Sharing

We do NOT:
- Sell your data to third parties
- Share your data with advertisers
- Use your data for purposes other than order synchronization
- Access your data except when providing support (with your permission)

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Disable the Extension at any time
- Clear local storage data via Chrome settings
- Request deletion of your data from Kolayxport
- Access all data we have stored about you

## Security

We implement security measures including:
- HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
- Local storage encryption via Chrome's security features
- Regular security audits of our code
- Minimal permission requests

## Children's Privacy

The Extension is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Posting a notice in the Extension update notes
- Displaying a notification in the Extension (for major changes)

## Third-Party Services

### Etsy
The Extension operates on Etsy.com. Your use of Etsy is subject to Etsy's own privacy policy and terms of service.

### Chrome Web Store
The Extension is distributed via the Chrome Web Store, subject to Google's privacy policies.
