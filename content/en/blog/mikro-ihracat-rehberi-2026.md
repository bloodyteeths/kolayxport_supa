If you ship small parcels abroad from Turkey through Etsy, eBay or your own site, three rules changed in the past year. Turkey raised its ETGB limits, the United States suspended its duty exemption for low-value parcels, and the European Union introduced a flat customs duty on parcels under €150. This post explains each change in plain terms, with sources.

> This post is general information, not legal or tax advice. Check your own situation with your accountant (mali müşavir) and your carrier.

## What are micro-export and ETGB?

Micro-export (mikro ihracat) means sending small, light export shipments abroad through an authorised express courier or postal operator using an **ETGB (Elektronik Ticaret Gümrük Beyannamesi, electronic commerce customs declaration)** instead of a full export declaration. The courier files the declaration on your behalf as an **indirect representative**, under an authorisation you give it ([Lires](https://www.lires.com.tr/post/mikro-ihracat-s%C4%B1n%C4%B1r%C4%B1-nedir-2026-kdv-iadesi-nas%C4%B1l-al%C4%B1n%C4%B1r), [BirFatura](https://birfatura.com/etgb-nedir-nasil-alinir/)). You don't need to hire a customs broker to file it, but you are responsible for the product description, HS code and value you give the courier.

## The 2026 limit: 600 kg and €30,000

For years the limit was **300 kg** gross and **€15,000** per shipment. Amendment **Series No. 5** to the communiqué on customs procedures for goods carried by post and express cargo (Series No. 1) was published in the **Official Gazette no. 33126 on 3 January 2026**. It raised the export limit that operators can declare to **600 kg** gross and **€30,000** ([KPMG Turkey](https://kpmgvergi.com/yayinlar/mali-bultenler/gumruk/posta-ve-hizli-kargo-yoluyla-tasinan-esyanin-gumruk-islemlerine-iliskin-tebligde-degisiklik-yapildi/3455), [Cumhuriyet](https://www.cumhuriyet.com.tr/ekonomi/posta-ve-hizli-kargo-gonderilerinde-yeni-degerler-aciklandi-agirlik-ve-parasal-ust-sinirlar-degisti-2466744)).

The provisions took effect on different dates:

- Most provisions, including the higher limit, took effect on publication, **3 January 2026**.
- Article 1, which requires operators to employ at least two licensed customs brokers or work with a firm of that capacity, took effect on **3 April 2026**.

For a seller shipping single Etsy or eBay orders, these limits may seem far away. The difference shows in bulk shipments: larger cartons to a warehouse, boutique or wholesale buyer abroad can now stay within ETGB. Confirm with your carrier whether your shipment qualifies.

## How to invoice a micro-export sale

You issue the invoice **to the foreign buyer**, not to the courier or the marketplace. It is issued as an e-Arşiv invoice (or e-Fatura, depending on your status) with **0% VAT** under the export exemption. The invoice usually carries the note "3065 sayılı KDV Kanunu'nun 11/1-a maddesi gereğince KDV'den istisnadır" ([Paraşüt](https://www.parasut.com/blog/ihracatta-e-fatura-earsiv-uygulamasi), [Lires](https://www.lires.com.tr/post/mikro-ihracat-s%C4%B1n%C4%B1r%C4%B1-nedir-2026-kdv-iadesi-nas%C4%B1l-al%C4%B1n%C4%B1r)).

In practice:

- **Match the invoice to the ETGB.** Description, quantity and amount should match what you gave the courier. VAT refund claims check this.
- **VAT refunds are possible.** VAT you paid on materials or stock can be refunded or offset against the export. Work through the paperwork with your accountant.
- **e-Arşiv scope changed for 2026.** e-document thresholds are updated regularly and changed again for 2026. Ask your accountant which documents you must issue electronically and from what amount.

## United States: de minimis is suspended

For years the US exempted shipments under $800 from duty (de minimis). That exemption has been **suspended for shipments from every country since 29 August 2025** ([KPMG](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/08/us-cbp-implements-eo-suspending-duty-free-de-minimis-treatment.html)). What happened next:

- In February 2026 the US Supreme Court struck down tariffs imposed under IEEPA. The ruling did not restore de minimis, because the suspension rests on a separate legal basis.
- In August 2026 the US Court of International Trade upheld the suspension ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/13/trump-trade-court-de-minimis-tariffs-ieepa.html), [Troutman Pepper Locke](https://www.troutman.com/insights/the-800-free-pass-is-gone-cit-confirms-president-can-eliminate-de-minimis-tariff-exemption/)).
- According to secondary sources, CBP made the suspension indefinite on 24 June 2026, and the statutory exemption is repealed from 1 July 2027 ([Dutylane](https://dutylane.com/tariff/policy/de-minimis)). Check both dates against official CBP notices.

What this means for you: low-value parcels to the US are now subject to duty and processing fees. The rate depends on your product's US tariff code (HTS). Don't assume a rate; look up your code in the [US HTS search](https://hts.usitc.gov/) or ask your carrier.

## European Union: €3 per item line, not per parcel

From **1 July 2026** the EU charges a **flat €3 customs duty** on consignments worth up to €150. It is charged per distinct tariff classification in the parcel: items in the same class pay one €3, items in different classes pay €3 each. The measure is temporary and runs until **1 July 2028**. The seller or importer declares and pays it ([European Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/ensuring-fairness-and-safety-eur3-customs-duty-low-value-parcels-2026-06-29_en)).

The duty does not replace VAT. VAT and IOSS rules still apply to EU sales; check whether your marketplace collects VAT on your behalf.

**A note on France:** France introduced a €2 per-item fee on small parcels from outside the EU on 1 March 2026 ([info.gouv.fr](https://www.info.gouv.fr/actualite/taxe-sur-les-petits-colis-comment-ca-marche)). It ended the fee on 1 July 2026, when the EU €3 duty started ([Banque des Territoires](https://www.banquedesterritoires.fr/la-france-met-fin-sa-taxe-sur-les-petits-colis-pour-saligner-sur-ses-voisins)).

## DDP or DDU: who pays the duty?

If the **buyer pays duty on delivery (DDU/DAP)**, they may refuse a parcel with an unexpected charge, and return or storage costs can come back to you. One Turkish seller described being asked for $488.31 in duty and fees on a $44 shipment after the buyer refused delivery ([Şikayetvar, 7 October 2026](https://www.sikayetvar.com/shipentegra/ship-entegradan-44-dolar-degerindeki-gonderi-icin-488-dolar-fahis-gumruk)).

If **you prepay duty (DDP)**, the buyer has no surprise, but you need to build the cost into your price or shipping fee. Whichever you choose, decide before orders arrive and state it clearly in your listing.

## Pre-shipment checklist

1. **Is the HS code right?** Set the six-digit international code for every product. A wrong code changes both the duty and the number of €3 lines in the EU.
2. **Is the commercial invoice description specific?** Write material and use, such as "100% cotton embroidered baby blanket", not "gift" or "textile product".
3. **Is the value real?** The declared value should match what the buyer paid.
4. **Have you decided DDP or DDU?** Ask your carrier what it offers and at what cost.
5. **Does your price cover the new costs?** Include duty, processing fees and currency spread. See our [Etsy profit calculation](/en/blog/etsy-kar-hesaplama) post.
6. **Have you told the buyer?** Mention possible customs charges in the listing and the order message.
7. **Is the invoice issued to the buyer at 0% VAT?** Check that it matches the ETGB data.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Turkey's micro-export limit in 2026?

600 kg gross and €30,000 per shipment. The limit was raised from 300 kg and €15,000 by the amendment published in the Official Gazette on 3 January 2026.

### Who files the ETGB?

The authorised express courier or postal operator files it on your behalf as an indirect representative. You supply an accurate description, HS code and value.

### Is VAT charged on a micro-export invoice?

No. The invoice is issued to the foreign buyer at 0% VAT under the export exemption. VAT you paid on the goods can be refunded or offset.

### Are US parcels under $800 still duty-free?

No. De minimis has been suspended for every country since 29 August 2025, and a US court upheld the suspension in August 2026. The duty rate depends on your product's HTS code.

### Is the EU €3 duty charged per parcel?

No, per tariff classification. A parcel with three items in different classes pays €9. It applies to consignments up to €150 from 1 July 2026 to 1 July 2028.
