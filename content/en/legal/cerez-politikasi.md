This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies that MODSONS YAZILIM E-TİCARET İTHALAT İHRACAT LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ ("KolayXport", "we") uses on the kolayxport.com website and in the KolayXport app. For general information on how we process personal data, see the [KVKK Privacy Notice](/en/kvkk-aydinlatma-metni) and the [Privacy Policy](/en/gizlilik-politikasi). The Turkish version of this policy prevails.

## 1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

A cookie is a small text file that a website saves in your browser. The browser's **local storage** and **session storage** store information in your browser in a similar way. In this policy we call all of these "cookies".

## 2. Cookie categories

We use cookies in three categories:

| Category | What it does | Consent needed? |
|---|---|---|
| **Necessary** | Lets you sign in, keeps your session secure, secures account connections and remembers your preferences. The service does not work without them. | No. Used under KVKK Art. 5/2-c (performance of a contract) and Art. 5/2-f (legitimate interest). |
| **Analytics** | Measures in aggregate how the site is used. | Yes, your explicit consent. |
| **Marketing** | Measures the results of our ad campaigns and helps us show ads to relevant audiences. | Yes, your explicit consent. |

## 3. Necessary cookies we use today

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `__Secure-next-auth.session-token` (`next-auth.session-token` in local development) | Cookie, HttpOnly | Keeps your session secure | Up to 30 days |
| `__Host-next-auth.csrf-token` | Cookie, HttpOnly | Protection against cross-site request forgery (CSRF) | Browser session |
| `__Secure-next-auth.callback-url` | Cookie | Returns you to the right page after sign-in | Browser session |
| `next-auth.state`, `next-auth.pkce.code_verifier` (with secure prefix) | Cookie, HttpOnly | Security of Sign in with Google | About 15 minutes |
| `ebay_csrf`, `amazon_csrf` | Cookie, HttpOnly | Security check while connecting an eBay or Amazon account | 10 minutes |
| `cookie-consent` | Local storage | Remembers your cookie choice | Until you delete it |
| `kolayxport_locale` | Local storage | Remembers your language (Turkish/English) | Until you delete it |
| `kx.*`, `kx_*` and similar app keys | Local / session storage | Remembers choices you make in the app (table columns, saved views, recent searches, dismissed notices, order sound setting, unsent drafts) on your device | Local storage: until you delete it; session storage: until the tab closes |

These cookies work only on KolayXport's own domain and are not used for advertising. App preferences in local storage are not sent to our servers.

## 4. Analytics and marketing cookies

As of this policy's effective date, the site **does not use** any analytics or marketing cookies. We plan to use these tools in future:

- **Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4** (Google LLC) — analytics,
- **Meta Pixel and Meta Conversions API** (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. / Meta Platforms, Inc.) — marketing,
- **X (Twitter) pixel**, **TikTok Pixel**, **LinkedIn Insight Tag** — marketing.

These tools run only **after you give explicit consent** to the relevant category. For Google tools we use Google Consent Mode v2: before consent, analytics and ad storage are set to "denied", and these tools' tags do not load without your consent. ## 5. Third-party services' own cookies

When you sign in with Google, pay on Stripe's checkout page, or are sent to a marketplace's sign-in page to connect it, those pages are on that company's domain and its own cookies and cookie policy apply. We do not set or read those cookies.

## 6. KolayXport Chrome extension

If you install the Chrome extension, it uses the browser's extension storage and the session cookie on the KolayXport domain to recognise your KolayXport session and keep its settings. The extension does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

## 7. How to change your choices

- **Cookie preferences panel:** The notice shown on your first visit lets you accept or reject each non-necessary category. You can change your choice or withdraw consent at any time through the **"Cookie Preferences"** link at the bottom of the site.
