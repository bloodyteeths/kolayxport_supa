## 2. The Service

KolayXport is subscription software delivered over the internet (SaaS), built especially for online sellers who sell abroad. Depending on your plan and the services you connect, it provides:

- **Listing management:** create and edit listings on Etsy, eBay, Amazon, Shopify, Wix, Trendyol and other supported channels; bulk editing; photo and video management; copying listings between channels. Your edits are saved as drafts in KolayXport first and sent to the marketplace only when you choose to send them (or at the time you schedule).
- **AI tools:** suggested titles, descriptions, tags, alt text and product images; research explanations; reply drafts for customer messages; and an AI assistant.
- **Research:** keyword, product, competitor shop and price-gap research; research indicators on listing pages through the browser extension.
- **Order management:** orders from connected channels in one list, pick lists and packing slips, order export, sending tracking numbers back to the channel.
- **Shipping:** shipping labels with your own FedEx, UPS, DHL eCommerce (MNG Kargo) or Shippo accounts, electronic commercial invoices, shipment tracking.
- **Export documents:** preparing the Excel file for ETGB (Turkish electronic export declaration for e-commerce) and e-mailing it to the customs broker you name; issuing e-invoices / e-archive invoices through your Paraşüt account.
- **Stock and finance:** optional multi-channel stock sync; sales, fee, ad spend and profit reports.

Features may change over time. Some features depend on what the relevant third party offers through its API and may not work the same way on every channel.
