## 2. Categories of personal data we process

Depending on how you use KolayXport, we may process:

- **Identity:** first and last name; when you sign in with Google, the name and profile photo on your Google account.
- **Contact:** e-mail address; the phone number and address you enter in your sender (shipping) profile.
- **Customer transactions:** subscription plan, trial period, usage counters (orders, labels, AI credits), support requests and correspondence, in-app settings and preferences.
- **Financial:** billing details, payment status, subscription and invoice history. Card details are collected and processed by Stripe; your full card number is not stored on KolayXport servers.
- **Professional / business information:** shop names, identifiers of the marketplace and carrier accounts you connect, product and listing content, order and finance records, the tax number and similar details you enter for export documents (for sole traders this information can be personal data).
- **Transaction security:** a hash of your password, session data, IP address, browser and device information, sign-in and activity logs, access tokens and API keys for the accounts you connect.
- **Marketing:** your cookie preferences; in analytics and marketing tools we plan to add, and only if you consent, online identifiers and ad interaction data (see the [Cookie Policy](/en/cerez-politikasi)).

As a rule we do not process special categories of personal data (KVKK Art. 6), and we ask you not to send us such data.

## 3. Purposes

We process your personal data to:

1. Create your account, verify your identity, and run e-mail verification and password reset.
2. Provide the service: connect your marketplace and carrier accounts, sync orders, edit and send listings, prepare shipping labels and ETGB files, produce finance reports, and run research and AI tools.
3. Manage subscriptions, the trial period, plan limits, payments and billing.
4. Answer support requests and send required service notices (for example security, payment or service changes).
5. Keep the service secure, prevent abuse and unauthorised access, and find and fix errors.
6. Meet legal obligations (tax, commercial, consumer and e-commerce law) and respond to requests from competent authorities.
7. Establish, exercise and defend legal claims.
8. Only with your consent: measure site usage, measure ad campaign performance, and send you commercial electronic messages.

## 4. Legal bases

We rely on these legal bases under KVKK Art. 5:

- **Formation or performance of a contract (Art. 5/2-c):** account, service, subscription and support (purposes 1–4).
- **Legal obligation (Art. 5/2-ç):** invoicing, keeping books and records, distance contract records, requests from authorities (purposes 3 and 6).
- **Expressly provided for by law (Art. 5/2-a):** records the law expressly requires us to keep.
- **Establishing, exercising or protecting a right (Art. 5/2-e):** disputes and claims (purpose 7).
- **Legitimate interest (Art. 5/2-f):** security logs, abuse prevention, debugging and improving the service (purpose 5), provided this does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms.
- **Explicit consent (Art. 5/1):** analytics and marketing cookies, ad measurement, commercial electronic messages, and certain transfers abroad described below (purpose 8). You can withdraw consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

## 5. How we collect data

We collect personal data partly or fully by automated means through:

- Sign-up, sign-in, settings and support forms, and e-mail correspondence.
- Information Google sends us when you sign in with Google (Google OAuth).
- Data from the marketplace, carrier and accounting accounts you connect, retrieved through their APIs with your authorisation.
- The KolayXport Chrome extension (only if you install and use it; see the Privacy Policy).
- Payment and subscription notifications from our payment provider Stripe.
- Server logs, cookies and browser local storage.

## 6. Transfers within Türkiye (Art. 8)

Limited to the purposes above and in line with KVKK Art. 8, personal data may be transferred to:

- **Domestic services you connect on your instruction:** marketplaces such as Trendyol, Hepsiburada, N11 and Çiçeksepeti; DHL eCommerce (MNG Kargo); Paraşüt. We transfer data so you can use these services on your own behalf.
- **Competent public authorities and courts:** where the law requires and on request.
- **Advisers:** accountants, auditors and lawyers, under confidentiality and only as far as necessary.

## 8. Retention

We keep personal data for as long as the purpose requires and for the periods set by law. Main periods:

## 9. Sellers' buyer data

For buyer data that arrives with orders from the shops you connect (buyer name, shipping address, phone, e-mail), **the seller is the data controller**. KolayXport is a **data processor** that processes this data only on the seller's behalf and instructions. If you bought from a shop and have a request about your data, please contact that seller first. We forward requests we receive to the seller concerned and give the seller the technical help it needs.

## 10. Your rights under KVKK Art. 11

You have the right to:

1. Learn whether your personal data is processed,
2. Request information about the processing,
3. Learn the purpose of processing and whether data is used for that purpose,
4. Know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom data is transferred,
5. Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data,
6. Request deletion or destruction under the conditions in KVKK Art. 7,
7. Request that steps under items 5 and 6 are notified to third parties who received the data,
8. Object to a result against you that arises from analysis exclusively by automated systems,
9. Claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.
