# Turkish Online Seller Pain Points (research snapshot, 17 Oct 2026)

Input for ad hooks, SEO keywords and landing pages. Sources are mostly 2025–2026. Where Şikayetvar shows no year, the post is from **2026**, because the site leaves the year off for current-year posts. Quotes are **verbatim**, with the sellers' own spelling and wording kept. Personal names, phone numbers and IBANs have been left out.

Status key (based on the product summary we were given, not checked in code): **solves**, **partial**, **no**, **needs check**.

## Segment A: Export sellers (Etsy, eBay, Amazon global, Shopify/Wix abroad)

### A1. Getting paid in Turkey: payouts bounce, get stuck or get lost
- **Frequency: High.** Seen: 2 detailed Şikayetvar Etsy payout complaints (139.000 TL piling up since August 2026; Sipay losing 2 transfers worth about 41.800 TL). 12 Payoneer complaint titles on page 1, mostly about verification or blocked accounts. Several Etsy Community "returned deposits" threads from Turkish sellers.
- Quotes:
  - "Etsy’de satıcı olarak yaptığım satışların ödemelerini 3 Ağustos’tan bu yana alamıyorum." ([Şikayetvar, 18 Aug 2026](https://www.sikayetvar.com/etsycom/etsy-odemelerim-geri-donuyor-ve-139000-tl-alacak-birikiyor))
  - "ödemelerim sistem içinde dönüp duruyor ama banka hesabıma ulaşmıyor." (same)
  - "Hem Etsy hem de Moka United tarafında aynı noktada tıkanmış durumdayım" (same)
  - "Sipay, çözüm sunmak yerine sürekli yeni belgeler talep ediyor ve Etsy’nin sağlamadığı dekontları istiyor." ([Şikayetvar, 22 Oct 2025](https://www.sikayetvar.com/etsycom/sipay-etsy-odemelerimi-banka-hesabima-yatirmiyor-kayip-paralarim-nerede))

## (3) Frequent pains we don't solve (product gap input)

1. **Hepsiburada (and N11/Çiçeksepeti/Amazon TR) stock and order sync.** Domestic sellers almost always list on Trendyol and Hepsiburada together, and integrator complaints name both. Without Hepsiburada, the "stok senkronizasyonu" promise is incomplete for Segment B. **Highest priority gap.**
2. **Payouts to Turkey** (Etsy deposits returned, Moka United/dLocal/Sipay, Payoneer verification). Very high pain and no product answer. A partner or content play (a guide to payout options) is possible.
3. **Account suspension or closure help** (Etsy, eBay, Trendyol fraud). This could be a checklist or "risk score" feature that flags policy-risky listings (keywords, "how it's made", duplicate images). Needs feasibility check.
4. **Domestic cargo labels** (Yurtiçi, Aras, Sürat, Hepsijet, Trendyol Express). Integrator users lose their "kargoya verildi" status sync.
5. **Duty/DDP calculator and IOSS** for US/EU after de minimis ended and the €3 EU fee arrived. This is new pain in 2025–26, and a tool or content would fit our label flow.
6. **Delivery-proof and lost-parcel claims** (tracking evidence for "teslim edildi ama gelmedi").
7. **Penalty and score monitoring** (late-shipment risk, disputed-return rate) for Trendyol and Hepsiburada.
8. **Human, reachable support.** This is the #1 meta-complaint about every incumbent. Even a fast WhatsApp/Turkish chat response SLA would be a differentiator (we have no WhatsApp).
9. **Return-abuse evidence** (packing video/photo log per order).

## (4) Methodology and limitations

- **Sources read directly** (browser, 17 Oct 2026):
  - Şikayetvar brand and complaint pages: Etsy (2 list pages), eBay, Trendyol (barem, partner, partner/reklam), Hepsiburada, N11, Sentos, Sopyo, Dopigo, BirFatura, Entegra, ShipEntegra and Payoneer. About 30 full complaints read.
  - Ekşi Sözlük topics "etsy üzerinden satış yapmak", "etsy", "mikro ihracat", "e-ihracat" and "trendyol satıcıları" (last 2–6 pages, filtered to 2025–2026).
  - Etsy Community "Etsy Türk Satıcıları" and "Etsy Türkiye Dijital ve POD" forums.
  - News and regulation from web search: ETGB limits, US de minimis, the EU €3 fee, TCMB FX support, and Etsy fees for Turkey.
- **Frequency** is a judgement based on how many relevant posts appeared on the first 1–2 listing pages of each source. It is not a statistical sample, and Şikayetvar skews toward angry, high-stakes cases.
- **Gaps:**
  - **Reddit** was blocked for both the browser and fetch, so there are no Reddit quotes.
  - **YouTube comments** did not load (the comment panel rendered blank), so only video titles were seen.
  - **Facebook groups** were not attempted (login-walled).
  - **Capterra/G2/Google reviews** returned only vendor-written comparison pages.
  - **Şikayetvar** started demanding an interactive CAPTCHA partway through. Per instructions it was not solved, so Hepsiburada and Amazon seller-side complaints are thin. Şikayetvar's Hepsiburada "satıcı" page is mostly buyer complaints.
  - **Amazon global seller pain** (related-account suspensions, verification) comes from secondary blog content only.
- **Quotes** are copied verbatim from the pages, including spelling, missing spaces and English loanwords. Very long passages are trimmed with "…". Ekşi links point to the topic, not the individual entry. The "mikro ihracat" Ekşi quote about FX support being "kaldırılmış" is the seller's perception; the support was restructured from 1 Oct 2026.
- **KolayXport status** relies on the product summary in the brief. Items marked partial or needs check (FedEx/UPS rate competitiveness, Trendyol settlement reconciliation, Trendyol listing-edit coverage, duty calculation) should be checked against the codebase before any ad claims them.
