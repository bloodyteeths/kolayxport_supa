## Reading this report (2026-10-17)

- **Before the rebuild** (production, same Lighthouse settings; see `../site-audit.md`): performance 56–73, LCP 6.2–8.9 s, contrast failures on 13 of 21 pages.
- **Now:**
  - Performance is 95–99 on all 41 public pages.
  - Accessibility, best practices and SEO are 100 everywhere.
  - CLS is at most 0.05 on every page but one (that post is 0.051).
- **Still open against the strict budget:** the *simulated* LCP is 2.1–2.9 s, versus the target of under 2.0 s.
  - Measured in real Chrome with 4× CPU slowdown and 1.6 Mbps / 150 ms (`.cache/lcp.mjs` method), LCP is 1.0–2.2 s.
  - The remaining simulated time is web-font swap plus hydration.
  - Next levers: inline critical CSS; preload only the Latin subset of the display face; render the hero H1 in the fallback font until the font is cached.

**What changed to get here:**
1. Page content no longer starts at `opacity:0`.
2. Both 750 KB message dictionaries are out of the shared bundle (namespace subsets per page; the app loads its locale on demand).
3. The app's provider tree is a separate chunk that marketing pages never load (`_app` went from 591 KB to 22 KB gzipped).
4. Responsive and AVIF screenshots, a phone crop and preload.
5. Self-hosted Latin + Turkish font subsets (about 28 KB each, previously about 160 KB).
6. No link prefetch in the site chrome.
7. Pricing is server-rendered without MUI, and its cards are ordered with CSS (CLS on mobile went from 0.31 to 0).
