## When ads bring clicks but the listing doesn't convince

When Etsy sales don't come, the first reflex is to raise the ad budget. But if clicks come and sales don't, the problem is often the listing itself: a title written in Turkish and then translated, tags buyers don't search for, a description that doesn't explain the product, or photos taken from a single angle.

## Listing optimization in KolayXport, step by step

**See where to start.** The listing doctor gives each of your listings an SEO score and compares it with similar listings from the same search. Even with hundreds of listings, you have a priority list.

**Rewrite the copy.** AI suggests an English title, description and tags for your listing. On Etsy, keywords always stay in English because your buyers search in English. On eBay you also get item specifics and a price suggestion. For Trendyol listings the suggestions are in Turkish.

**Strengthen the photos.** Generate new product images with AI from the listing editor and have descriptive alt text written for every photo. For applying the same photo operation across many Etsy listings, there's the bulk photo studio.

**Work in bulk.** Apply AI to the listings you select in the bulk editor. You see the results in the grid and fix anything you don't like.

**You stay in control.** Every suggestion stays a draft first. The preview shows how the listing will look in Etsy or eBay search. When you're ready, press Send, or schedule the changes for a time you choose.

## A small tip for a good start

Rather than changing hundreds of listings on the same day, start with the 10–20 listings that get the most views but few sales. Read the suggestions, remove phrases that don't really describe your product, then send. After a few weeks, check the same listings again in the listing doctor and move on to the next group. Etsy takes time to evaluate changes, so steady, measured progress works better than rewriting everything daily.

## What we don't do

- We don't guarantee sales.
- No AI writing for Shopify and Wix products.
- The bulk photo studio is Etsy-only, part of Pro, and needs a wide screen.
- Bulk tag adding from the listing doctor isn't available yet.
- There's no AI chat assistant; AI works through the buttons inside the listing editor.
