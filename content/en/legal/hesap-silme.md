You can permanently delete your KolayXport account and all data linked to it yourself, at any time. This page applies to the website and to the KolayXport Android app on Google Play.

## How to delete your account

1. [Sign in](/login) at kolayxport.com or in the KolayXport Android app.
2. Open **Settings › Preferences**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Type your account e-mail address to confirm. If you sign in with e-mail and password, enter your password too.
5. Tap **Delete account permanently**. You are signed out and brought back to this page.

If you cannot sign in, e-mail destek@kolayxport.com from the address registered in your account with the subject "Delete my account". We complete the request within 30 days after verifying it is you.

## What is deleted

- Your account details (name, e-mail, password hash, preferences)
- Connected stores and marketplace access tokens
- Orders, buyer details, shipping labels and shipments
- Listings, drafts, uploaded images and videos, AI-generated content
- Your research, report and notification records

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. The data leaves our rotating server backups within about 3 months at most.

## What is kept

- An active paid subscription is cancelled when the account is deleted; you are not charged again.
- Invoice and payment records we must keep by law are kept with our payment provider for the required period (up to 10 years).
- Aggregate usage statistics without personal data (for example total AI cost) may be kept.

Your listings and orders on marketplaces (Etsy, eBay, Amazon, etc.) stay in your account on that marketplace; KolayXport does not delete them.
