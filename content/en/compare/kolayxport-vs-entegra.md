## In short

Entegra is a wide-ranging integrator covering Turkish marketplaces, carriers, accounting, warehousing and fulfilment. According to its [package page](https://www.entegrabilisim.com/paketler), packages are priced annually, e-invoice credits are sold separately, and the e-export package that includes Etsy (Temu, Etsy, Ozon) sits in Package 5 at ₺125,000/year (VAT included). Entegra also offers AI product adding, aimed at Turkish marketplaces.

KolayXport focuses on selling abroad: single and bulk editing of Etsy and eBay listings (eBay listings created in Seller Hub excepted), AI for English titles and tags, keyword and product research, and international FedEx/UPS labels. It has no Hepsiburada, no N11 and no warehouse management, and domestic labels only through DHL eCommerce (MNG).

## Who should pick which?

**Entegra may suit you better if:**
- you sell in volume on several Turkish marketplaces,
- warehouse management, fulfilment or an accounting connection is a must for you.

**KolayXport may suit you better if:**
- most of your sales are on Etsy and/or eBay,
- you want to research and rewrite your listings for English-language search,
- you'd rather pay monthly than a year up front.

**Using both:** keep domestic channels in Entegra and manage Etsy and eBay listings in KolayXport. Turn stock sync on in only one of them so two tools don't write the same product's stock.

The Entegra details were checked on Entegra's package page on 17 October 2026; prices and package contents change. For KolayXport's current plans, see the [pricing page](/en/fiyatlandirma).
