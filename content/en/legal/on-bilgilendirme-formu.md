This form informs buyers before they buy a KolayXport subscription, as required by Article 5 of Turkish Consumer Protection Law No. 6502 and the Distance Contracts Regulation (Mesafeli Sözleşmeler Yönetmeliği). At checkout you are asked to confirm that you have read it; the version you confirm is e-mailed to you once the contract is formed. The Turkish version is the binding text.

## 1. Who this form applies to

The consumer rights in this form apply to individuals or legal entities who buy the Service **for purposes outside their trade or profession** ("consumers"). KolayXport is software for businesses that sell online; if you buy the Service for your business (for example your marketplace shop), Law No. 6502 does not apply to you and our relationship is governed by the [Terms of Service](/en/kullanim-sartlari), the Turkish Code of Obligations No. 6098 and the Turkish Commercial Code No. 6102. The price, scope and cancellation information below still applies.

## 3. Main characteristics of the Service

KolayXport is e-commerce management software delivered over the internet on a subscription (SaaS). Depending on the plan, it includes listing editing and publishing, AI-assisted content suggestions, market research, multi-channel order management, shipping labels, ETGB file preparation, stock sync and finance reports. The Service is not a physical product; nothing is delivered, and it is provided electronically through your account.

The scope and usage limits of each plan (monthly orders, labels, connected shops, AI credits) are shown on the [Pricing](/fiyatlandirma) page and at checkout.

**Functionality and compatibility:** The Service needs an up-to-date web browser and an internet connection. Some features require you to connect your own marketplace, carrier or accounting account; these third-party accounts and their fees are not included in the subscription. The Chrome extension works only in Google Chrome and compatible browsers.

## 4. Price and payment

- The fee is the **total amount** shown at checkout for the plan (Starter, Growth or Pro) and period (monthly or yearly) you choose.
- Payment is taken in advance by credit or debit card through our payment provider Stripe. The seller does not store your card details.
- New accounts start with a 14-day free trial with no card required; there is no charge for the trial. If you buy a plan while your trial is still running, the first charge may be taken at the end of the trial; checkout will say so.
- There are no delivery costs.

## 5. Term, renewal and termination

- The subscription is formed for the period you choose (1 month or 1 year) and **renews automatically** for the same period unless you cancel; the renewal fee is charged to your saved card.
- You can cancel at any time in Settings › Plan & Billing or by writing to destek@kolayxport.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period; you are not charged for the next period.
- The minimum contract term is one billing period you choose.
- Price changes apply to an existing subscription only from the next renewal, with at least 30 days' notice.

## 6. Performance

The Service is made available electronically in your account immediately after payment is confirmed.

## 7. Right of withdrawal

For service contracts, a consumer may withdraw from the contract within **14 days** of the day the contract is formed, without giving any reason and without paying a penalty.

**When the right of withdrawal does not apply:** Under Art. 15/1-(ğ) of the Distance Contracts Regulation ("services performed instantly in electronic form") and Art. 15/1-(h) ("services whose performance has started, with the consumer's approval, before the withdrawal period ends"), the right of withdrawal cannot be used. At checkout you are therefore asked to tick an unticked box to confirm explicitly that **you ask for the Service to start immediately, before the withdrawal period ends, and that you therefore lose your right of withdrawal**. If you give this confirmation, you have no right of withdrawal for the subscription period whose performance has started.

If you do not give this confirmation, or no exception applies, you can send your notice of withdrawal within the 14-day period by one of these routes:

- E-mail: destek@kolayxport.com (subject: "Notice of withdrawal")
- In writing: Beyazevler Mah. 80053 Sok. Tanrıkulu Apt. No:8 İç Kapı No:02, 01170 Çukurova / Adana, Türkiye
- KEP: modsonsyazilim@hs01.kep.tr

We refund the total amount you paid within **14 days** of receiving your notice of withdrawal, using the payment method you used and at no cost to you.

## 8. Complaints and disputes

Send complaints to destek@kolayxport.com or the postal address above. In a dispute, consumers may apply to the **Consumer Arbitration Committee** where they live or where the transaction took place, within the monetary limits announced each year by the Ministry of Trade, and to the **Consumer Court** for disputes above those limits. Applications can also be made through the Consumer Information System (TÜBİS) on e-Devlet.
