## "134 products, 4 sales" is usually a visibility problem

Etsy buyers search in English. If your title reads like a translation from Turkish, half your tag slots are empty, or your tags just repeat the words in your title, the listing won't surface in search. Ads don't fix that. You get clicks, the listing doesn't convince, and ROAS stays near 1.

That's why KolayXport starts with your listings. The **listing doctor** gives each listing an SEO score and compares it with similar listings from the same search. It suggests fixes where something is missing, and you can move the AI-written title into a draft with one click.

## Rewriting your listings no longer eats your weekend

AI suggests an English title, description and tags for each listing. Keywords stay in English because your buyers search in English. When you'd rather not go one by one, the bulk editor lets you change titles, prices, quantities and tags for hundreds of listings in a grid.

Every change stays a **draft** first. Nothing reaches Etsy until you press Send. If someone changed the listing on Etsy in the meantime, the send stops and tells you. If you made a wrong edit, you can roll back to an earlier version. Want the same listing on eBay? Copy it to an eBay draft in one click.

## Orders live in the same workspace

Your Etsy orders appear in the same list as your other marketplaces. Connect your own FedEx or UPS account and print international labels from the order. The commercial invoice travels electronically with the label; you only need an HS code and a description for each item.

## What we don't do

- We don't guarantee sales. We help you make your listings easier to find.
- Software can't fix Etsy payouts that fail to reach Turkey or shop suspensions.
- Etsy buyer addresses and tracking upload need the Chrome extension, which isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet.
- Etsy messages aren't shown in the app.
- FedEx and UPS need your own carrier account; your rate is whatever you've agreed with the carrier.
