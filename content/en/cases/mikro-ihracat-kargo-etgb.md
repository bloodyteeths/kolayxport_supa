## International shipping isn't just about price, it's about paperwork

Volumetric weight surprises, unexpected deductions and checking the charge after every shipment are everyday problems for a lot of sellers. For a seller with their own FedEx or UPS account, the real workload is paper: the label, the commercial invoice, HS codes, and the list that goes to the broker for ETGB.

## How an international shipment is prepared in KolayXport

Your Etsy, eBay and Amazon orders come into the same list. Once you've entered your own FedEx or UPS API details, you print the label from the order. The commercial invoice is sent electronically with the label: ETD for FedEx, EDI for UPS. All you provide is an HS code and an English description per item. For UPS, IOSS and VAT numbers are checked before the shipment is created.

With many orders, select them all on the bulk label screen. Domestic orders go to DHL eCommerce and international ones to FedEx or UPS; labels print one after another, and if something fails you can stop and retry. Then print the pick list and packing slips.

For ETGB, an Excel file is built from the international orders you select and emailed to the customs broker you've set in Settings. You can send up to 100 orders at a time.

## Getting set up

Fill in your sender profile completely before printing labels; the company name, address and phone number on the label come from there. Entering your products' HS codes and English descriptions correctly once saves time on every later shipment. Don't forget to set the broker's email address for ETGB in Settings.

## What we don't do

- We don't file the ETGB; we prepare the file and send it to your broker.
- We don't sell shipping or promise discounted rates. FedEx and UPS need your own account.
- We don't track FedEx and UPS shipment status in the app, and labels are voided on the carrier's portal.
- We don't calculate duties, DDP or IOSS.
- No Yurtiçi, Aras, Sürat or PTT labels; domestically there's only DHL eCommerce (MNG Kargo).
- Etsy's own shipping labels (Etsy Postage) aren't supported.
