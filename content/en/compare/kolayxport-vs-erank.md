## In short

eRank is a research and SEO tool. Its [pricing page](https://erank.com/pricing) shows a free plan and paid plans from $5.99 a month. It offers keyword research, competitor tracking, listing audits and an AI listing helper, with keyword data covering Amazon, eBay and Printify as well as Etsy. There is a browser extension. The pricing page doesn't include order management, shipping labels, Trendyol or a Turkish interface.

KolayXport puts research next to the rest of the work: you can write the keyword you found into the listing, keep the change as a draft and send it to Etsy, and handle orders and FedEx/UPS labels in the same app. The interface is in Turkish and English. There's no free plan, and for someone who only wants research it costs more than eRank.

## Who should pick which?

**eRank may suit you better if:**
- you only need keyword and competitor research and edit your listings on Etsy yourself,
- you also look at Amazon or Printify keyword data,
- your budget is small or you want to start on a free plan.

**KolayXport may suit you better if:**
- after researching, you need to update hundreds of listings in bulk,
- you want orders, labels and listings in one app with a Turkish interface,
- you sell on eBay or Trendyol as well as Etsy.

**Using both:** if you research in eRank and bulk-edit in KolayXport, the two tools don't get in each other's way.

The eRank details were checked on eRank's pricing page on 17 October 2026. For KolayXport's current plans, see the [pricing page](/en/fiyatlandirma).
