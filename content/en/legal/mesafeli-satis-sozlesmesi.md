## 2. Subject

This agreement sets out the parties' rights and obligations for the provision of, and payment for, the KolayXport subscription service (the "Service") that the Buyer buys electronically at kolayxport.com, under Turkish Consumer Protection Law No. 6502 (the "Law") and the Distance Contracts Regulation (the "Regulation").

For Buyers who buy the Service for business or professional purposes, the consumer rights in the Law and the Regulation do not apply; for them, the agreement applies within the [Terms of Service](/en/kullanim-sartlari) and general law.

## 5. Term, renewal and termination

5.1. The agreement is formed for the period chosen by the Buyer and renews automatically for the same period at the end of each period unless the Buyer cancels. The renewal fee is charged to the Buyer's saved payment method.

5.2. The Buyer may cancel at any time in Settings › Plan & Billing or by notice to destek@kolayxport.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period; the Buyer keeps using the Service until then and is not charged for the next period. Subject to the Buyer's rights under the Law, no partial refund is given for the unused part of a period that has started.

5.3. The Seller gives at least 30 days' notice of price changes by e-mail; the new price applies from the next renewal. The Buyer may decline the new price by cancelling before renewal.

5.4. If the Buyer materially breaches the Terms of Service or does not pay, the Seller may suspend the Service or terminate the agreement as set out in the Terms of Service.

## 6. Obligations of the parties

6.1. The Seller provides the Service in line with the characteristics stated in the pre-contract information and the Terms of Service.

6.2. The Buyer confirms that the information given at checkout is accurate and agrees to use the Service lawfully and in line with the Terms of Service.

6.3. If providing the Service becomes impossible after the agreement is formed for reasons outside the Seller's control (for example a third-party API shutting down), the Seller will notify the Buyer within 3 days of learning of it and refund the fee for the period that cannot be performed within 14 days.

## 7. Right of withdrawal

7.1. A Buyer acting as a consumer may withdraw from the agreement within **14 days** of the day it is formed, without giving any reason and without paying a penalty.

7.2. **Exception:** Under Art. 15/1-(ğ) (services performed instantly in electronic form) and Art. 15/1-(h) (services whose performance has started, with the consumer's approval, before the withdrawal period ends) of the Regulation, if the Buyer ticked the unticked box at checkout to confirm explicitly that **they ask for the Service to start immediately and therefore lose the right of withdrawal**, the right of withdrawal cannot be used for the subscription period whose performance has started. If this confirmation was not given, the right of withdrawal can be used under clause 7.1.

7.3. Notice of withdrawal is given within the period by e-mail to destek@kolayxport.com, by KEP to modsonsyazilim@hs01.kep.tr, or in writing to the address in clause 1.

7.4. The Seller refunds the total amount charged within **14 days** of receiving the notice of withdrawal, using the payment method the Buyer used and at no cost to the Buyer.

## 8. Disputes

8.1. This agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Türkiye.

8.2. A Buyer acting as a consumer may apply to the **Consumer Arbitration Committee** where they live or where the transaction took place, within the monetary limits announced each year by the Ministry of Trade, and to the **Consumer Court** for disputes above those limits.

8.3. For Buyers who are not consumers, **the courts and enforcement offices of Adana** have jurisdiction.

## 9. Evidence and notices

Correspondence between the parties takes place through registered e-mail addresses. The Seller's electronic records are evidence under Art. 193 of the Turkish Code of Civil Procedure; the Buyer keeps the right to prove otherwise.
